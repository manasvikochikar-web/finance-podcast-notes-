In this section, I will learn how to file my Income Tax Return (ITR) by myself. I will start from the basics and understand what an ITR is, who needs to file it, and why it is important. I will learn how to choose the right ITR form, enter my income details, claim eligible deductions, check my tax calculation, and submit the return online. The main goal is to understand the process step by step so that I can confidently file my own ITR instead of depending completely on someone else.
